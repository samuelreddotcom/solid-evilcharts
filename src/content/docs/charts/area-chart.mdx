## Usage

```tsx
<EChartsAreaChart data={data} config={config} xDataKey="month">
  <EChartsAreaChart.Grid />
  <EChartsAreaChart.XAxis dataKey="month" tickFormatter={(v) => v.slice(0, 3)} />
  <EChartsAreaChart.YAxis />
  <EChartsAreaChart.Tooltip />
  <EChartsAreaChart.Legend />
  <EChartsAreaChart.Area dataKey="desktop" variant="gradient">
    <EChartsAreaChart.Dot variant="border" />
  </EChartsAreaChart.Area>
</EChartsAreaChart>
```

## Fill variants

`<Area variant>` selects the fill. `gradient` is the default.

<ApiTable>
  <ApiRow name="gradient" type="fill">Series colour fading to transparent.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name="gradient-reverse" type="fill">The same fade, inverted.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name="solid" type="fill">Flat fill at a fixed alpha.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name="dotted" type="fill">Dot pattern. Canvas only.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name="lines" type="fill">Diagonal line pattern. Canvas only.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name="hatched" type="fill">Cross-hatch pattern. Canvas only.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name="none" type="fill">Stroke only, no fill.</ApiRow>
</ApiTable>

<Callout variant="warn" title="Three fills need the canvas renderer">
  `dotted`, `lines` and `hatched` are drawn with a 2D pattern, so they need a
  real canvas context. Under `renderer="svg"` they fall back to no fill rather
  than erroring — worth knowing if you switch renderers and a chart goes flat.
</Callout>

## Stacking

`stackType` on the root controls how several areas combine.

<ApiTable>
  <ApiRow name='"default"' type="StackType">Areas overlap, drawn back to front.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name='"stacked"' type="StackType">Values accumulate.</ApiRow>
  <ApiRow name='"expanded"' type="StackType">Accumulate and normalise to 100%.</ApiRow>
</ApiTable>

## Parts

<ApiHeading>&lt;Area&gt;</ApiHeading>

<ApiTable>
  <ApiRow name="dataKey" type="string" required />
  <ApiRow name="variant" type="AreaVariant" default='"gradient"' />
  <ApiRow name="strokeVariant" type='"solid" | "dashed" | "animated-dashed"' default='"dashed"'>

  <ApiRow name="isClickable" type="boolean" default="false" />

    Renders the last segment as a dashed, fill-less buffer.
  </ApiRow>
</ApiTable>

<ApiHeading>&lt;Dot&gt; and &lt;ActiveDot&gt;</ApiHeading>

Nested in an `<Area>`. Omitting the element is `"none"`; including it bare is `"default"`.
